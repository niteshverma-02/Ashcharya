# Method 1b — Personality dials

Palette and layout are not enough: two products with different colours still look like twins if they share
the same density, type scale, corners, borders and label style. Every identity therefore sets **six dials**.
Set them from the users and the context, write them on the identity card, and let them drive the tokens.

## The dials

| Dial | Low end | High end | Set it from |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Density** | spacious: 16–18px body, 52–64px rows, big gaps | dense: 13px body, 32–40px rows, tight gaps | how many items users scan at once vs. how fast they act on one |
| **2. Scale contrast** | flat: titles 1.3× body | dramatic: hero numbers 4–6× body, titles 2.5× | is there ONE number/action that matters most? |
| **3. Shape** | sharp: 0–4px radius, square chips | soft: 16–28px radius, pill everything | material (steel, paper, glass bottle, fabric) |
| **4. Edges** | borderless: separation by spacing and tone | ruled: hairlines everywhere, like a form or ledger | do users think in forms/registers, or in objects/cards? |
| **5. Voice** | quiet: sentence case, no caps labels, muted colour | loud: condensed caps, bold blocks of colour, big labels | environment noise and urgency |
| **6. Ornament** | none: pure type + colour | rich: texture, marks, illustrations, photos | how often the screen is seen (daily tool → less) |

Motion energy follows dial 5: quiet → fades only; loud → snappy springs and counters.

## Consequences (so the dials really change the look)

- **Typography follows dials 1, 2, 5.** Pick a face whose *character* matches: quiet → humanist sans or a
  serif; loud → condensed grotesk; precise → mono-heavy. Don't reuse the same eyebrow style (tiny tracked caps)
  in every product — it is a *loud/ruled* choice, not a default.
- **Components follow dials 3, 4.** Tabs can be underlines, pills, folder tabs or segmented blocks; chips can
  be outlined, filled or plain text with ×; tables can be ruled grids (ruled) or zebra rows with no rules
  (borderless — still one value per column).
- **Spacing follows dial 1.** Write the base unit (4 / 6 / 8px) and the row height on the card.
- **Surfaces follow dials 4, 6.** Paper with hairlines, flat colour fields, or cards on a tinted canvas.

## Example settings (same structure, different products)

| Product | Density | Scale | Shape | Edges | Voice | Ornament | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Harvest POS (source) | dense | medium | medium 12–16 | ruled | medium (caps eyebrows) | rich | crafted counter tool |
| Pharmacy billing counter | medium | **dramatic** (huge total) | soft | borderless | **loud** | none | big, bold, fast, legible at arm's length |
| Loan file review | **spacious** | flat | **sharp** | ruled (like a form) | **quiet** | light (paper, stamp) | calm print-like reading room |
| Cold-chain control room | **dense** | medium | sharp | borderless (tiles by tone) | loud (status) | none | instrument panel |

## Sibling test, extended
Two products must differ on **at least three dials** *and* in palette structure or archetype. If they don't,
they will read as the same UI even with different colours.
